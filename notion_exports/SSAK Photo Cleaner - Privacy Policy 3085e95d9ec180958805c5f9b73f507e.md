## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to SSAK: Photo Cleaner (the “App”).

## 2. Summary

SSAK: Photo Cleaner is designed to process your photo-cleaning actions primarily on your device.

We do not operate a user account system for this App.

## 3. Data We Access or Process

The App may access or process the following categories of data:

1. Photo library access data (to show, sort, trash, restore, and delete photos).
2. Local app settings and local app state (for example: language, haptics/sound settings, trash queue metadata, free-delete quota state, premium entitlement flag).
3. Purchase-related verification data provided by platform billing systems (for purchase/restore handling).
4. Ad/reward event data handled by ad SDK providers (for rewarded ads, when used).

## 4. How Data Is Used

Data is used only to:

1. Provide core photo-cleaning features.
2. Save app preferences and local app state.
3. Process and restore in-app purchases.
4. Provide rewarded ad functionality and related anti-abuse checks.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

The App may use third-party platform/SDK services that process limited data under their own policies, including:

1. Apple App Store (in-app purchases on iOS).
2. Google Play Billing (in-app purchases on Android).
3. Google AdMob (rewarded ads, if used).

## 6. Data Retention

1. Local app data remains on your device until you delete app data or uninstall the App.
2. Platform purchase and ad providers retain data according to their own policies.

## 7. Your Choices

You can:

1. Revoke or change photo permissions in your device settings.
2. Delete local app data by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.
3. Manage purchases through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law in your region.

## 9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a local-first mobile app architecture.

## 10. Contact / Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the official Support URL shown on the App’s store listing.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The latest version will be made available through the App and/or the linked public policy page.
