### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. I accidentally deleted a photo! Can I recover it?

**Yes, absolutely!** SSAK does not permanently delete your photos immediately.
Please go to the default **Photos app > Albums > Recently Deleted**.
You can recover your photos there within 30 days.

### 2. I purchased "Lifetime Access" but features are still locked.

Please tap the **'Restore Purchase'** button in the app settings or on the payment screen. This will sync your purchase history without charging you again.

### 3. How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled directly by Apple. Please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://reportaproblem.apple.com) to request a refund. As a developer, we do not have access to your payment details.

### 4. Is my privacy safe?

**100%.** SSAK processes all photos locally on your device. We do not upload or store your photos on any external servers.
