**💡 FAQ**

- **Cannot restore purchase?** : Go to Settings > Tap the 'Restore Purchase' button.
- **Tracking seems inaccurate?** : GPS signals can be weak indoors or underground, which may cause data errors. Please ensure you are outdoors.
